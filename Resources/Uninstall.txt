Removing Crest

1. Open Crest Settings. Turn off Open Crest at login. In Connections, choose Remove integration for Claude if you installed it. This preserves unrelated Claude hooks and restores the previous status-line configuration when available.
2. Quit Crest from its menu-bar menu, then move Crest.app to the Trash.
3. For a reinstall, keep local data. To remove it, move ~/Library/Application Support/Crest to the Trash after checking it for anything you need. This folder holds encrypted clipboard history, tray references, bridge events/helper and integration backups. Removing the folder does not delete the originals referenced by the tray.
4. App preferences are stored under the bundle identifier app.crest.mac. They include the selected folder bookmarks, display preference and enabled features. Remove these only if you want a completely fresh setup.
5. To remove the clipboard encryption key, use Keychain Access and locate the generic password with service app.crest.clipboard and account local-v1. Deleting that key makes any retained encrypted clipboard archive unreadable. Keep the key if you may reinstall or need to recover the archive.
6. Optional: revoke Crest’s Calendar, Automation, Bluetooth or Accessibility access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Do not delete the clipboard key to solve a read failure. Keep the encrypted archive and key together while troubleshooting. Development builds with a changed ad-hoc signature may require macOS to ask for Keychain access again.

Uninstalling cannot undo copies or AirDrop transfers you previously made outside Crest.
